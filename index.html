Appearance

Selena is a slender young woman with long, flowing black hair that features vibrant cyan-blue streaks and inner highlights. Her eyes are a deep violet-purple, though some depictions show them as blue depending on her frame. She is intrinsically linked to music and is often portrayed with her cello or Bowstring Rapier.[reference:0]

Personality

Gentle, kind, and deeply devoted to art, Selena carries a quiet resilience. Despite the trauma she has endured, she remains compassionate and finds solace in music. She is known for her maturity and calmness in operations, though those close to her can glimpse moments of solitude and depression.[reference:1]

Her Story

From opera stage to the battlefield — a journey of loss and rebirth.

The Rising Star of Babylonia

Once an opera singer and artist, Selena was born in Babylonia and received an aristocratic-style education. Her beautiful voice and musical talents made her the most anticipated rising star in the World Government Association of Art (WGAA).[reference:2]

The Space Station Incident

During a battle at a space station, Selena went missing. She was saved by an "agent" and began a long journey on Earth to retrieve her memories. At the end of that journey, she found herself again and returned to Babylonia as a new person.[reference:3]

Transformation and Trauma

Selena suffered prolonged exposure to a Hetero-core shard, and her body was restructured by the Red Tide. Her M.I.N.D. was severely damaged, and she endured phantom pain even after regaining her memories. She became a Transcendant before eventually returning to Babylonia.[reference:4][reference:5]

Rebirth as Pianissimo

The Pianissimo frame was created based on Capriccio, specifically adjusted to suit Selena's habits and to alleviate the traumatic phantom pain in her M.I.N.D. It was co-developed by the Science Council and the WGAA.[reference:6][reference:7]

The Meaning of "Pianissimo"

Selena chose the name "Pianissimo" together with the Gray Raven Commandant. It serves as a reminder that the hall of art welcomes everyone, and that her cello will always sing for everyone she encounters. The name became the pillar that helped her stand up again and again in the storm.[reference:8]

Hidden Secrets

The white floral vine pattern on her frame is a gift from the most important person to Selena. Iris is her favorite flower. In the past, she could only see it in a test tube, but during her journey on Earth, she finally saw them in full bloom after rain.[reference:9]

Selena can "hear" the remaining "sounds" of the Red Tide and use their power to fight. However, this causes a heavy load on her M.I.N.D., forcing Pianissimo into a "Noise state" after a certain period. Once healed, she can harness the power again.[reference:10]

The powerful computing power of Pianissimo's core enables Selena to play five parts simultaneously. Her latest interest is performing symphonies in the concert hall as a one-man band, serving every role from each part to the conductor.[reference:11]

Gameplay

Pianissimo is an S-Rank Physical Attacker who dominates the battlefield with sound.

🎵 Musical Notes

Accumulate Musical Notes to enhance her physical damage output. Her core passive revolves around managing these notes for devastating attacks.

🎻 Bowstring Rapier

Her signature weapon directs the suppression device to play a song that creates overwhelming sound waves, sweeping across the battlefield like a storm.

❄️ Glaciation

Pianissimo deals Glaciation damage, freezing enemies and controlling the flow of combat with her icy melodies.

🐺 Snow Waltz (CUB)

Her CUB, "Snow Waltz," is an audio companion capable of sound wave attacks and playback. It has become one of her creators at her music studio.
